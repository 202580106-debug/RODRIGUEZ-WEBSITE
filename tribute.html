Remembering Bobet

Rene Clert "Bobet" Baterbonia
Student-athlete, son, big brother, Mindanao's pride.

His Story

Bobet grew up near the Agusan Marsh in Talacogon, Agusan del Sur. He was the second of seven children, raised by his parents Rene Sr. and Rovelyn.

Through hard work and discipline, he rose from his hometown courts to the national stage. His journey showed many Filipino families what is possible when talent meets perseverance.

A dream that reached far beyond the horizon.

Highlights

  • Ateneo de Davao University He played for the school throughout his student life, balancing the classroom and the court.
  • Palarong Pambansa 2025 Named MVP and a member of the Mythical Five.
  • ASEAN School Games, Brunei Represented the Philippines on the international stage.
  • Ateneo Blue Eagles Committed to the Ateneo de Manila University men's basketball program, one of the country's brightest young prospects.
  • June 8, 2026 Bobet passed away at age 19 in a drowning incident in Dipaculao, Aurora, during a team-building activity.
  • June 24, 2026 He was laid to rest in his hometown of Talacogon, Agusan del Sur.
  • Senate Resolution The Philippine Senate adopted a resolution honoring his life, legacy, and contributions to Philippine sports.

Why We Carry His Name

Bobet's story is about hope, family, and effort. We hope every jar reminds you to work hard, stay kind, and share what you have.
