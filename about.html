Our Story

Peanut Baterbonia started as a school project with one goal: make peanut butter that is simple, tasty, and easy to love.

We believe a good spread needs only a few things. Great peanuts. Careful roasting. A little patience.

The rest is up to you. Toast, banana, rice cake, or straight from the spoon.

What's in the jar

  • Roasted peanuts
  • A pinch of salt
  • Flavor add-ins in select jars (honey, cocoa, chili)
  • That's all.

Why "Baterbonia"?

We chose this name to honor Rene Clert "Bobet" Baterbonia, the Ateneo and Palarong Pambansa standout from Talacogon, Agusan del Sur, who passed away in June 2026 at age 19.

His story is one of hard work, family, and hope. We want our name to remind people of those same things.
